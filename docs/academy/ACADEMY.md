# The Academy

The university pane: your physics bachelor at the University of Vienna
(curriculum 2026, 180 ECTS). This page says how to read it and how to use it.

## The astrolabe, step by step

Read it like a clock face.

**1. The rings are semesters.** Ring i is the one closest to the eye in the
middle, ring vi the outermost. The small numerals i to vi at 12 o'clock, just
left of the pointer, name them.

**2. Each ring is cut into arcs, and an arc is one module.** A module is a
block of the curriculum you pass as a whole, like *Analysis 1* or
*Experimentalphysik 1*. Its label is its code and its ECTS: `ANA1 8` means
Analysis 1, 8 ECTS.

**3. An arc is as long as its ECTS.** Each ring is one semester's worth of
modules going once round. A module with 8 ECTS out of the semester's 30 fills
8/30 of the circle; a 2 ECTS module is a sliver.

**4. The arcs start at 12 o'clock and go clockwise**, in the order the
curriculum lists the modules. Your first semester, ring i, reads:

| From | Module | ECTS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 o'clock | STEOP1, Experimentalphysik 1 | 8 |
| then | STEOP2, Physikalische Rechenmethoden | 8 |
| then | STEOP3, Forschung und Studium | 2 |
| then | LA1, Lineare Algebra 1 | 4 |
| back to 12 | ANA1, Analysis 1 | 8 |

**5. How an arc is drawn tells you where you stand with it:**

| Looks like | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Solid, bright band | Passed: every course of the module is graded 1 to 4, and together they carry its full ECTS. |
| Band of moving grain | This term: you have added a course of it and it has no grade yet. |
| Thin double outline | Open to you: a module of the semester after yours. |
| Faint dotted trace | Not yet. |

**6. The pointer is the term.** The line from the eye to the rim with a star
at its tip turns once per term: at 12 o'clock on 1 October, back at 12 on
31 January (1 March to 30 June in summer). It says how far the term has run,
not which module you are on. Drag anywhere on the plate to turn it by hand; it
springs back. The tilted ring and the dots riding on it are the rest of the
old instrument, and are only there for the look.

**7. The eye in the middle is the StEOP**, the entry phase every Vienna degree
starts with. You have to pass it (STEOP1 to 3, 18 ECTS) before most of
semester ii opens.

**Hover an arc** to get a card with the module's name, the ECTS you have
earned in it, and each of its courses with its grade. **Click an arc** to mark
it: its books lean out of the shelf and its dates light up in the week. Click
it again to let go.

## The shelf of books

Every course you add becomes a book. A module usually has two courses, a
lecture (VO) and its exercise class (UE); each is its own book.

- **Upper shelf:** this term's courses. **Lower shelf:** courses from other
  terms. It only appears once you have some.
- **Order:** left to right in the curriculum's order, so the same order as the
  arcs. A **brass bookend** stands between one module's books and the next.
- **Size:** a book is as thick as its ECTS. A lecture (VO) stands taller than
  an exercise class (UE).
- **Spine:** the module's short name (EP1, RM, LA1...) runs up it, the type
  (VO, UE) sits at the top, and once graded, a **seal with the grade** sits
  at the bottom. A passed book turns pale, a failed one dark.
- **The tag under each group of books** names the module.

**Hover a book** to see its full name, type, ECTS and grade. **Click it** to
take it down. The card that opens lets you:

- **seal its grade:** 1 sehr gut, 2 gut, 3 befriedigend, 4 genügend, 5 nicht
  genügend (a fail). Click the same grade again to take it back.
- **change the module it counts toward:** needed for electives, which the
  server can only guess.
- **pick your exercise group:** UE courses have several groups meeting at
  different times; choose yours once you are told, and its dates join the week.
- **shelve it** (put it back) or **strike it** (remove it).

## Adding your courses

On an empty shelf, **add the first term** adds all eight first-semester courses
in one go.

To add any other course you need its **u:find number**, the six-digit course
number the university gives every course:

1. Open [ufind.univie.ac.at](https://ufind.univie.ac.at) and search the
   course's name, or browse the course directory (*Vorlesungsverzeichnis*) to
   *Bachelor Physik*.
2. Every course is listed with its number first, for example
   `261608 VO Analysis 1`. That `261608` is the number. The same number is
   shown on the course in u:space.
3. Type it into **add a course by its u:find number** and press **add**.

The course is added to the current term. The server reads its name, type,
ECTS, module, every group's dates and rooms, and any exams u:find has posted,
and guesses the module from the codes u:find lists. It asks u:find again
once a day, or when you press **ask again**; the grades, groups and modules you
set are kept.

## The rest of the pane

- **The tally:** ECTS passed out of 180, this term's ECTS, the StEOP out of 18,
  your ECTS-weighted grade over passed courses, and the next exam.
- **Exams ahead:** the next exams u:find lists for your courses or their StEOP
  module, with the days left. Hover one to mark its module.
- **The week:** this week's dates for your courses (lectures, and the
  exercise groups you picked), with a line at the current hour.
- **The rector's armillary:** six beads on its tilted ring, one per semester.
  A bead lights as its semester's modules are passed; the current one pulses.

## The lore

Vienna's university was founded in 1365, and its first chairs were paid to
watch the sky. The registrar here keeps the old habits: every course is a book
on a shelf, every grade a wax seal, and the degree is read off an astrolabe,
the way Miskatonic kept its restricted stacks. Kael'thos approves. He says the
mathematics is the telescope, and that the eye at the pole is there because
the first thing a degree teaches you is how to look.
