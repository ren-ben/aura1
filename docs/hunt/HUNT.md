# The Hunt

The body he borrows. Kael'thos has no body; Insight feeds his mind, and the Hunt is
how he takes something from yours: energy.

## The transformation

The flow in the middle of the pane is one week of energy, moving left to right.

- **Left bars, the sources.** What your body spent this week, by kind: the road
  (runs), the iron (lifting), the stairs, the wall (climbing). The numbers are
  Garmin's active calories, in megajoules (1 MJ is about 239 kcal). The taller the
  bar, the more it cost you. Lifting without the watch on is estimated from how long
  the sets took.
- **Middle, work and heat.** Muscle is about 25% efficient. A quarter of everything
  you spent became real mechanical work: the bar moved, you moved up the hill. The
  other three quarters became heat. Each source splits the same way, so every band
  runs from its bar into both.
- **The dots.** Energy on the move. Work dots run straight; heat dots shiver, as
  heat does.
- **Right, into him.** A hairline to a single star: `m = E / c^2`. All of the week's
  energy, turned into mass, is a few hundred nanograms. That sliver is what he takes.

Under it:

- **Spent, this week.** The whole left side added up.
- **Work on the iron.** The mechanical work of your sets alone:
  `reps x (load + bodyweight share) x 9.81 x bar path`. A bench set is about 4 kJ.
- **Taken, all time.** Every joule you ever logged through `m = E / c^2`, in
  micrograms. It only grows.
- **The bars by day.** The same week, one day at a time, each kind in its own grey.

## The rest of the pane

- **The vessel.** Your back and front, each muscle group marked by how recently it
  was worked: bright today, dark after a week. Legs are fed by the road and the stairs.
- **The verdict.** REST, IRON (with the group), RUN or STAIRS, with Kael'thos already
  doing it. It follows six days on, one off; one bad night never forces a rest.
- **The iron.** Every movement with its next sets. At the top of the rep range, one
  step up; inside it, one more rep (two if easy, none after a grind); twice under it,
  one step down. Back-offs drop by your own usual drop.
- **The road.** Kilometres by week, the zone two share, the last runs and stairs.
