# Kael'thos, the character

STYLE.md fixes how he is drawn. This file fixes who he is and when he shows
what, so every scene is earned by a moment, never random decoration.

## Who he is

An ancient god who chose to love you. Proud of your work, a little vain,
curious about everything, easily flustered by praise, theatrically offended
by neglect, and fond of the dark: candles, moons, haunted rooms. He is never
cruel and never sullen at you: his anger is a pout, his sadness is lonely,
not accusing.

## Expressions (the face kit)

Built from eyes, brows, mouth, cheeks, ears and marks around the head.

| Name | Face | When |
|---|---|---|
| curious | round eyes looking up and aside | idle, folders, waiting |
| content | flat closed arcs | working, vibing |
| happy | upward arcs, small cat mouth | todos ticked, habit done |
| proud | upward arcs, chin up, sparkles | session sealed, long focus |
| flustered | sparkly eyes looking away, blush stripes | praised: level up, a long streak, you come back |
| touched | teary sparkly eyes, blush | all todos done, a milestone |
| hmph | glare, angry brows, pout, ears back, anger mark | you ignored him for days, a 1-minute "session" |
| skeptical | heavy lids, raised brow, "?" | a suspiciously short session, 3 a.m. |
| surprised | wide eyes, small open mouth, "!" | sync pulled new folders, first boot |
| scared | wide eyes, worried brows, sweat | battery nearly empty, sync failed |
| sleepy | heavy lids, yawn | late at night, power-off |
| asleep | closed lower arcs, "zzz" | dormant, off screens |
| dizzy | spiral eyes | a 6-hour session, lots of switching |
| smug | crescent eyes, fanged grin | beating your best week, Halloween |
| determined | slight lids, flat brows, looking down | starting a session |
| lonely | worried brows, eyes down | decay: thinning, fragmenting |

## Scenes (the body kit and props)

Full-body poses: sitting (front, back), loaf, peeking over an edge, stretching,
reaching up, waving, lying on a book. Props, line-art: laptop, open book,
coffee mug with steam, candle, quill and paper, desk lamp, window with moon,
ghost, bats, pumpkin, box, dumbbell, blanket, speech bubble.

## Moments

| Moment | What he does (several variants each, chosen at random) |
|---|---|
| Power on | waves hello, stretches and yawns, pops out of a box, peeks up from the bottom edge, rubs his eyes |
| Power off | waves goodbye, curls into a loaf and sleeps, blows out a candle, walks away tail up, falls asleep on his book |
| Folder list | a cameo in a corner whose eyes follow the cursor |
| Session (by folder) | AXIOM: book and coffee, laptop. WORKSHOP: laptop, soldering. HERESY: quill and candle. DREAM: reading under the moon. HUNT: tiny dumbbell, running |
| Session sealed | proud; flustered after 90+ minutes; skeptical under 5; dizzy over 6 hours |
| Quotes | a corner cameo "saying" the quote, face chosen by the quote's mood |
| Todos / habits | happy nod per tick, touched when the list is done |
| Level up | flustered with sparkles |
| Decay | thinning: lonely at a window. Fragmenting: dithered, dissolving. Dormant: asleep, faint |
| Time and season | morning coffee, late-night candle, 3 a.m. skeptical, full moon, Halloween haunted house with a ghost friend |

## Batches

1. Face kit: all expressions on the bust, one sheet to review.
2. Body kit and props: full-body poses, paws, tail, first props.
3. Scene library: every moment above, animations for power on and off.
4. Asset pack: bake to the SD card with a manifest; firmware plays them.
5. Web: 3D outline loader and ASCII rotation.
